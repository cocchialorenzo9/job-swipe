# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people who are new to code, and keep feedback about
the work. Harassment, insults and sharing someone's personal information are not allowed, here or in any
project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately the same way as a security issue (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)):
GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** form, or an issue titled "Security contact request" with no details. The
maintainer reviews every report, keeps it confidential, and may warn, remove content from, or ban anyone who breaks
this code, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
