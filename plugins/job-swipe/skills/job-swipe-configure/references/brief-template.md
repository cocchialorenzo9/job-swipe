# Search brief template

Write the brief in the person's own language, as short bullets, only with things they said or their CV shows.
Leave a heading out if there is nothing for it. Keep it under ~250 words — it is read by every search run and
shown (and edited) by the person on their board.

```
## Roles I want
- <titles, in order of preference; include close variants worth searching>

## Roles to avoid
- <titles / kinds of work they don't want>

## Level
- <years of experience, current level; how big a step up is OK, e.g. "up to Senior, not Lead/Head of">

## Where
- <cities / countries; remote or hybrid preference; open to moving? where to, and on what condition>

## Languages
- <languages they can work in, with level; e.g. "English fluent; German B1 — skip jobs needing fluent German">

## Companies
- <kinds they like (size, industry, mission); companies to skip, incl. ones that rejected them>

## Must-haves
- <salary floor, visa sponsorship, team language, max office days, etc.>

## Deal-breakers
- <anything that makes a job a hard no>

## About me (for matching)
- <2–4 bullets: current role, years, strongest skills/tools, domains>
```
